# QR-menu and QR/contactless-ordering tools (non-POS) selling to Indian restaurants, cafes and stalls: pricing and features, 2025-2026

Method note (read first): direct page fetches of vendor sites (menutiger.com, finedinemenu.com, menubly.com, gloriafood.com, qrseva.com, dotpe.in, petpooja.com) were blocked in this research environment (DNS/egress-proxy denial). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited URLs, not from reading full pages. Prices are as reported by those summaries; many come from third-party directories or competitor blogs and they often disagree with each other, which is noted where it happens. Treat all prices as "verify on vendor page before quoting."

Currency conversion used throughout: **US$1 = ₹95.95** (actual rate quoted for 16 Sep 2026 on [longforecast.com](https://longforecast.com/usd-to-inr-forecast-2017-2018-2019-2020-2021-indian-rupee)). Other snapshots: ₹94.84 on 8 Sep 2026 ([dollarrupee.in](https://dollarrupee.in/)); ₹96.96 on 27 Jul 2026 (same search). I could not reach an RBI reference rate; conversions are approximate (±2%). INR figures exclude 18% GST unless stated.

## Q1. Tool-by-tool profiles: origin, India presence, free vs paid plans, limits, features, fees, apps, claims

### Takeaway
Global QR-menu SaaS (MenuTiger, FineDine, Menubly, Uniqode, QRCodeChimp, IAMenu, Mobi2Go) price in USD at roughly ₹750-₹13,000/month and mostly lack Indian payment rails (no confirmed Razorpay/UPI). Indian players split into (a) commission/fee-per-order models (DotPe, Thrive, Orderzy-paid-by-diner) and (b) a long tail of small flat-fee Indian startups priced ₹99-₹999/month (DineCard, Ahaar Scan, MenuScan, Menuthere, QRSeva, MenuManager, Scanmyfood), which are the closest direct competitors to a ₹199-₹459 product.

### Inferences
- The global tools' entry prices in rupees (Menubly Pro ≈₹767-959, MenuTiger Regular ≈₹1,631, FineDine Starter ≈₹2,783) are 3-14x our QR Starter ₹199, require USD card billing, and do not offer UPI natively; they are weak direct competitors for Indian stalls but strong on design, languages and analytics.
- The real price war is with Indian flat-fee startups at ₹99-₹399/month (DineCard, QRSeva, Menuthere, MenuScan, Ahaar Scan); Ahaar Scan's ₹2,999/yr (≈₹250/mo) with UPI + dashboard + WhatsApp + loyalty is the closest feature/price analogue to our Starter/Growth tiers.
- Commission-model players (DotPe, Thrive) win when the stall has low volume, since zero fixed fee beats ₹199/mo below a certain order volume (e.g., at 3%, ₹199 equals ₹6,633 of monthly GMV) — calculation, not a cited fact.

## Q2. Cheapest credible way for an Indian food stall to get (a) QR menu only, (b) QR ordering with dashboard, (c) ordering + online payment

### Takeaway
(a) Free: Canva/Google Drive PDF + any QR generator, or a free-tier tool (QRSeva, Menuthere, Plota, MenuTiger Freemium, Menubly Free). (b) Free-with-limits (MenuTiger Freemium: 2 tables, 49 items, 200 QR orders/month) or diner-fee/commission models (Orderzy, Thrive dine-in, DotPe); cheapest flat paid ~₹250-399/mo (Ahaar Scan). (c) Ahaar Scan ₹2,999/yr with UPI next-day settlement is the cheapest flat-fee option found; otherwise commission models (Thrive 3% delivery/takeaway, DotPe ~1-3%) or GloriaFood's 2% online-payment fee (but Indian gateway unconfirmed).

## Q3. Free tools and their catch; feature gaps both ways vs our product

### Takeaway
"Free" always carries a catch: tight caps (MenuTiger 2 tables / 49 items / 200 orders), vendor branding and no custom domain (Menubly Free), payment-processing fees (GloriaFood 2%), commission (Thrive 3% off-premise, DotPe ~1-3%), diner-paid platform fees (Orderzy), or manual re-keying (WhatsApp catalogue, PDF menus). Competitors commonly offer multi-language menus, analytics, WhatsApp ordering, loyalty/coupons, custom branding/domain and AI menu import that we lack; most low-cost menu tools lack kitchen display, KOT printing, staff roles, token numbers and UPI-UTR matching that we have.
