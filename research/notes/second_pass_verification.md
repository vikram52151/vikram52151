## Q1. Thrive (thrivenow.in): shut down, or still selling ₹299/₹899 plans?

### Takeaway
**Resolved: Thrive announced its shutdown in December 2024.** The about.thrivenow.in pricing page quoted in the first round is a legacy page and should not be treated as a live 2026 offer. Thrive said its restaurant-side units (Direct, ONDC, Marketing Suite) would be handed to "industry partners", but no buyer or successor product was found. Drop Thrive from the live price comparison and use it only as a cautionary precedent for commission-model players.

### Cited Findings
- YourStory reported in **December 2024** (URL path /2024/12/) that the "food delivery startup Thrive to shut down operations amid intense competition" (third-party) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2024/12/food-delivery-startup-thrive-to-shut-down-operations); mirror [cwv.yourstory.com](https://cwv.yourstory.com/2024/12/food-delivery-startup-thrive-to-shut-down-operations)
- Co-founder and CEO Krishi Fagwani announced the shutdown on LinkedIn, citing a "lack of resources" and a market "dominated by a few well-funded giants". The company shut the Thrive Consumer App and said the **ONDC, Direct and Marketing Suite businesses are being moved to other industry partners**, with payments and tax compliance continuing "without disruption during the transition period" (third-party) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/coca-cola-backed-foodtech-startup-thrive-shuts-operations); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/thrive-exits-food-delivery-market-as-competition-heats-up/484322)
- Founded in 2020 by Fagwani, Dhruv Dewan and Karan Chechani. Jubilant FoodWorks took 35% (2021) and Coca-Cola 15% (2023). The company claimed 14,000+ restaurants in 80 cities (third-party) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/coca-cola-backed-foodtech-startup-thrive-shuts-operations); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/thrive-exits-food-delivery-market-as-competition-heats-up/484322)
- An afaqs headline reports that Dhruv Dewan moved to Tata Digital (headline only; the search tool could not corroborate it) — [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/brands/thrive-calls-it-a-day-after-5-years-dhruv-dewan-moves-to-tata-digital-9335904) (unverified detail)
- No 2025–26 report names who took over Thrive Direct or the Marketing Suite (search returned nothing) — gap, see below
- The Razorpay App Store and Restroworks marketplace still list a "Thrive Now" integration (9,500+ restaurants, 76 cities). Both pages are undated and appear to be stale listings (third-party) — [Razorpay App Store](https://razorpay.com/app-store/thrive); [Restroworks marketplace](https://www.restroworks.com/marketplace/thrive/)

### Inferences
- The first-round pricing (dine-in free, 3% delivery/takeaway, 2% + tax gateway, ₹299/₹899 plans, ₹349–1,249 marketing suite) is **the pre-shutdown rate card**. The help article cited in the first round was already ~4.5 years old, which fits a frozen site.
- Thrive is still useful as evidence: a backed (Jubilant, Coca-Cola), 14k-restaurant, ~3%-commission direct-ordering player could not sustain itself.

### Gaps
- MCA/Tracxn company status, Play Store last-update date for the Thrive partner app, and whether about.thrivenow.in still resolves could not be checked (fetches blocked; no search snippet).
- Identity of any partner that took over Thrive Direct or Marketing Suite: not found.

Oracle's primary notice was not retrieved directly. For planning, use 31 Mar 2027 as the conservative date. Either way, GloriaFood is not a live option for a new Indian restaurant in 2026.

## Q6. Swiggy InResto (Torqus) and Zomato: do they offer QR table ordering to restaurants now, and at what cost?

### Takeaway
**No 2025–26 evidence was found that either offers a restaurant-facing QR table-ordering product with a public price.** Torqus presents itself as "inresto + Torqus" (POS, KOT, online ordering), but no QR table-ordering page or price surfaced. Zomato's dine-in activity has moved to the **District** app (launched Nov 2024), which is a consumer discovery and pay app, not a table-ordering SaaS. The only dine-in fee evidence is NRAI's 2022 complaint of **4–12% commission** on Zomato Pay / Swiggy Dineout transactions.

### Cited Findings
- Dineout acquired inResto (dashboard for reservations, delivery, takeaway, feedback, loyalty); no QR table ordering mentioned (third-party, older) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=26324)
- Zomato's District app launched Nov 2024 and passed 6.5M downloads by Jan 2025, positioned for dining, events and entertainment (third-party) — [Storyboard18](https://cms.storyboard18.com/?p=54008); [Storyboard18 launch](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/zomato-to-launch-district-app-in-4-weeks-deepinder-goyal-45677.htm)
- In 2025 Zomato delivery commission was reported at 10–28% plus gateway and GST. Eternal was exploring commission changes, with no agreement as of Sept 2025 (third-party, delivery not dine-in) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/eternal-explores-changes-zomato-commission-114442407.html); [Restaurant India](https://www.restaurantindia.in/article/how-zomato-commission-impacts-restaurant-profit-margin-the-hidden-costs-every-restaurant-owner-must.16307)
- NRAI advisory (2022): Zomato Pay required restaurants to pay 4–12% commission on transactions via its gateway (third-party, dated) — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/zomato-pay-swiggy-diner-may-adversely-impact-restaurant-industry-in-the-long-run-nrai/) (first-round citation)

### Inferences
