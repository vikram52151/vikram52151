# Indian Restaurant POS / Billing Platforms: Entry ("Lite") Plans, Pricing and QR Ordering (2025–2026)

**How this was researched, and its limits (read first):** Research date 2026-10-12. The sandbox's egress proxy **blocked direct page fetches** of every vendor and aggregator domain tried (petpooja.com, restroworks.com, ristaapps.com, dotpe.in, softwaresuggest.com all returned 403 or DNS failure). Every finding below therefore comes from **web-search result snippets** of the cited pages, not full page reads. Labels used:
- **official**: the snippet came from the vendor's own domain.
- **third-party**: from an aggregator (Techjockey, SoftwareSuggest, Capterra, G2, SaaSWorthy), a reseller (IndiaMART, TradeIndia) or the press.
- **unverified**: from a **competitor's marketing blog** (DineOpen, Restrofi, Bill Feeds, OrderIt, Servyn, Billzova, Billboox), from a single source, or from data older than 2024.

Many of the "comparison" blogs that rank well for these queries are run by rival POS startups, so they are biased. Prices should be confirmed on the live vendor page before anything is published.

Pages checked (via search) per vendor are listed in the Cited Findings.

## Q1. Competitor profiles: entry plan, price, inclusions, add-ons, limits

### Takeaway
None of the established full-stack POS vendors (Petpooja, Restroworks/POSist, Rista/DotPe, Torqus/Inresto) publishes a public price list. Every price for them is third-party or reseller data.
- **Petpooja** is the reference point. Its base plan costs about ₹10,000 + 18% GST per outlet per year in year 1 (₹11,800), then about ₹7,500 a year from year 2. A cheaper "POSS Lite" tier, reportedly about 6,000 + GST a year, includes only "limited" Scan & Order.
- **Billing-only apps** (Vyapar, myBillBook, Swipe, Khatabook) are cheap, at ₹0 to about ₹5,000 a year, but none was found offering QR table ordering.
- **Thrive (thrivenow.in)** is the closest direct-ordering analogue. Dine-in QR is free, it charges 3% on delivery and takeaway orders, the payment gateway costs 2% + tax, and paid plans are ₹299 and ₹899 a month.

#### Payments / UPI MDR context (relevant to all vendors)
- **Current rule:** zero MDR on UPI and RuPay debit has applied since January 2020.
- **Reported new framework from 15 Oct 2026:**
  - 0.4% MDR on P2M UPI transactions above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above.
  - Small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month via UPI QR stay at zero MDR.
  - Merchants must not pass MDR on to customers.
  - **Not confirmed against an official RBI or NPCI notification.** Sources: [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/upi-charges-on-transactions-over-2000-from-15th-october-what-new-mdr-framework-means-for-consumers-and-merchants/81663.html); [NewsOnAir (Govt)](https://newsonair.gov.in/finance-ministry-clarifies-no-charges-on-person-to-person-upi-transactions/)
  - A 2025 report cited a ₹50,000 a month small-merchant threshold instead. — [MediaNama 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/03/223-govt-weighs-reinstating-mdr-on-upi-rupay-transactions/)
- **Gateway fees are separate:** zero MDR applies at the network layer, but payment gateways can still charge platform fees. Thrive, for example, charges 2% + tax on gateway payments. — [Razorpay blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/upi-mdr-for-merchants-in-payment-gateway-explained/); [Thrive pricing](https://about.thrivenow.in/pricing)

### Inferences
- **Petpooja's real entry price for QR ordering** is probably the Lite tier, at about ₹6,000 + GST (≈₹7,080) a year with "limited" Scan & Order. Full Scan & Order is reportedly bundled in Growth, at ₹20,000 + GST (≈₹23,600) a year. Lite's exact limits on Scan & Order are not publicly documented.
- **DotPe/Rista and Thrive monetise by commission or gateway fee, not subscription.** Their "free" dine-in QR is not free at volume once gateway charges (about 2%) and any online-order commission apply.
- **A pure-UPI-intent product avoids gateway fees,** because payment goes directly to the merchant's UPI ID at zero MDR for small merchants. That is a real cost advantage over gateway-based QR ordering, but it depends on the 2026 MDR rule details.
- **Enterprise vendors are irrelevant to a ₹199–459/month food stall buyer.** Restroworks, eZee Optimus and (on the Techjockey figure) Rista are priced several times higher and sold through sales calls.

### Gaps
- The official petpooja.com/pricing, restroworks.com/pricing, ristaapps.com/pricing and dotpe.in pages could not be fetched (proxy 403). Petpooja's current official Lite price, and the exact limits on its "limited" Scan & Order, are unconfirmed.
- No standalone price was found for Petpooja's Scan & Order add-on, or for GoFrugal or Rista QR-ordering add-ons.
- DotPe's current commission rate or rate card was not found from any primary source.
- Status and pricing of Swiggy Dineout or InResto's current POS and QR dine-in product were not found. Zomato Base's current status is also unknown.
- Neither Paytm's (2020) nor PhonePe's restaurant QR ordering could be confirmed as current.
- Outlet, user and order limits were not found for any vendor's entry plan, except Rista's 2022 free licence (200 products, 50 transactions a day).
- The contract lock-in terms for Petpooja and Restroworks are reportedly annual, but this was not verified from contracts.

## Q2. Cheapest way for a small stall or cafe to get QR menu + QR ordering, and year-1 all-in cost

The cheapest established-vendor QR-ordering routes are commission or gateway-fee models: Thrive dine-in at ₹0 + 2% gateway, and DotPe "free-to-start" with commission. ## Q3. What established POS vendors offer that a ₹199–459/month pure-QR product does not, and pricing transparency

### Takeaway
Full POS vendors (Petpooja, Rista, Restroworks, GoFrugal, eZee) differentiate on:
- GST-compliant billing,
- KOT printing and captain apps,
- inventory and recipe costing,
- Zomato/Swiggy aggregator integration and reconciliation,
- offline billing via a local server,
- deep reporting (80+ reports),
- loyalty/CRM,
- multi-outlet HO management,
- 24×7 support.

Pricing transparency is poor among incumbents: Petpooja, Restroworks, Rista/DotPe and InResto all route buyers to "contact sales". Billing apps (Vyapar, myBillBook, Swipe) and new QR-first entrants publish prices.

### Cited Findings
- **Petpooja features:** 150+ integrations, 80+ reports, 24×7 multilingual support. — [Petpooja blog](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/best-restaurant-pos-software-india/)
  - Offline billing and KOT on a local server, a Captain App for table orders, and KDS, kiosk and reservation add-ons. — [Petpooja Offline POS](https://www.petpooja.com/offline-pos)
  - Billing operations: split payments, merge tables, discounts. — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/petpooja-restaurant-management-platform/)

### Inferences
- **Where a pure-QR product is weak:**
  - **GST tax invoices** (compulsory for GST-registered outlets).
  - **KOT thermal printing** (many kitchens still want paper).
  - **Zomato/Swiggy order consolidation.**
  - **Offline continuity.**
  - **Inventory.**

  For a tiny stall that is not GST-registered and not on aggregators, these matter far less.
- **Opaque pricing is an exploitable wedge.** Pricing ambiguity from Petpooja, Restroworks, Rista and DotPe, plus reports of hidden hardware, AMC and add-on costs, creates a "transparent flat ₹199" positioning opportunity. Several QR-first startups (DineOpen ₹299, Restrofi ₹699) already use this exact messaging.
- **Positioning as complementary:** because Petpooja Lite already bundles "limited" Scan & Order with aggregator integration at about ₹7,000 a year, a pure-QR product could position as a complement (QR layer on top of existing billing) or as a stall-first, no-GST-needed option, rather than as a POS replacement.

### Gaps
- No vendor-confirmed language list beyond Petpooja (English, Hindi, Arabic per Capterra).
- Support model (onboarding visits vs remote) and contract lock-in terms are not documented in accessible sources.
- No YouTube review pricing was captured; it is not searchable via this tool.
