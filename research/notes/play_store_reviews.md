# Play Store / App Store listings and user reviews: Indian restaurant POS, QR ordering and billing apps

> **Method note (read first).** Research date: 12 Oct 2026. Direct fetching of play.google.com, apps.apple.com, AppBrain, AppGoblin, APKPure, Capterra and Reddit was **blocked** in this environment (DNS/proxy refusal for WebFetch and curl; Reddit is blocked even for search). Every figure below therefore comes from **search-engine-indexed snapshots** of the Google Play / App Store / AppBrain pages, which the search tool read and summarized. Consequences:
> - Ratings, review counts and install tiers are **snapshots of uncertain date** and sometimes disagree between hl=en_IN and hl=en_US pages, or between Play and AppBrain. Where they differ, both are given.
> - Most review snippets came back **without a visible review date**. Where a date or year was available, it is stated. Treat undated quotes as "visible on the listing in 2025–2026 snapshots", not as dated evidence.
> - Short quotes in double quotes are the review text as returned by the search tool. Everything else is a paraphrase.
> - App size and device requirements were not available in any snapshot. That gap applies to every app.
> - Re-verify the headline numbers by opening the Play URLs (hl=en&gl=IN) in a normal browser before publishing them externally.

## Q1. Per-app listing facts and review themes

### Takeaway
The big Indian restaurant-tech brands have high Play ratings: Petpooja about 4.5, Zomato Restaurant Partner about 4.5, Vyapar about 4.8 and myBillBook about 4.4. Their written negative reviews are still dominated by **poor or slow support, pushy sales, refund refusals and charges owners did not expect** (ads, onboarding fees, plan renewals). Small-developer restaurant POS apps (Zobaze, Vyapar "Table") get criticised for **KOT printing gaps, printer compatibility, split payment and subscription glitches**. Dedicated QR-menu apps on the Indian Play Store are tiny: no India-focused QR-ordering app with large installs and many reviews turned up.

"Restaurant QR Menu" (`com.iqonic.qrmenu`): 1.9★, 500+ downloads (a template) — [Menusa](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.menusa.android&hl=en_US); [QR Menu Maker](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.qrmenumaker)
- **MenuTiger** has no Play/App Store rating to report; it is a web SaaS. Third-party (competitor-written) comparisons say the free plan is capped at 1 store, 10 tables, 200 QR orders and 250 online orders a month, and 7 categories × 7 items, with paid plans at about US$17/month. One roundup scores it 7.5/10 and calls the interface more complex than simpler tools. Another says "those monthly fees add up quickly" — [MenuTiger](https://www.menutiger.com/); [qrmenugenerator.io](https://qrmenugenerator.io/blog/best-menutiger-alternatives-2026); [Tabres](https://tabres.com/articles/top-5-qr-menu-tools-for-restaurants)

### Inferences
- **Headline star ratings mislead.** Petpooja (4.5), Zomato Partner (4.5) and Vyapar (4.8) look excellent, but the same companies score far lower on iOS (Zomato 2.7–3.7, Swiggy Owner 1.9, DotPe 3.4, Vyapar about 3.5). Their written negative reviews cluster on support and money. High Play averages are probably inflated by in-app rating prompts at happy moments. The written 1–2★ reviews are the better signal.
- Most POS vendors (Petpooja, Restroworks, Rista, GoFrugal) **sell through sales teams**, and their Android apps are companion or owner apps. The Play Store is not their acquisition channel, which is why their install counts are modest (Rista 100K+, Petpooja 500K+). A self-serve Play listing is a real gap that our product can fill.
- **The QR-ordering app category on Play Store India is thin and unbranded.** No QR-first vendor app with large installs and many reviews surfaced. Our vendor app would compete mainly with generic "Restaurant POS / KOT billing" apps (Zobaze, Vyapar Table, BoltBills, etc.) for search terms.

### Gaps
- App size and Android version requirements were not available for any app, because the Play pages could not be fetched.
- Exact review dates were mostly unavailable. Only these were dated: the Petpooja refund (Dec 2025), the Zobaze printer complaint (Aug 2026), and Zobaze KOT requests (late 2025 to 2026).
- No Android "Petpooja Partner", "Rista Captain", Torqus POS, GoFrugal RestoBiz or OrderEasy listing was found. Restroworks app ratings and installs were not visible.
- Reddit owner threads could not be searched (domain blocked for this tool).
- Install tiers for Swiggy Partner, Khatabook-billing and the small KOT apps (BoltBills, RasaBill, Arow, My Rasoi, InstantMenu) were not captured.

## Q2. Cross-app recurring pain points (what small food businesses hate)

### Takeaway
Ranked by how often and how widely they recur:
1. Unresponsive or looping support.
2. Money surprises: unauthorised ad spend, onboarding fees, refusal to refund, forced plan upgrades, licence lockouts.
3. Sales pressure and over-promising.
4. Printer and KOT unreliability.
5. Bugs, and updates that remove things owners use.
6. Reporting errors, especially split payments.
7. Order notifications and outlet-online status failing.

Offline mode is a marketing promise everywhere, but reviews rarely confirm that it works.

### Inferences
- The strongest emotional triggers in 1★ reviews are about **money and being ignored**, not missing features. A transparent ₹199–₹459 plan with no setup fee, no auto-upsell calls, a clear trial end date, a no-questions cancellation and refund promise, and a guaranteed in-app or WhatsApp support response time would answer the top complaints directly.
- **Update regressions are a trust risk.** The Petpooja case shows that owners notice when a glanceable "today and yesterday sales" number moves. A home screen with sales totals that do not move between versions is valued.
- **Notification reliability** is a must-have for a Capacitor vendor app. Zomato and Swiggy reviews show that missed order alerts are rage-inducing. On Android, background push and audible alerts on budget phones need hardening (battery optimisation, foreground service).
- **Printer support** is the most common hardware pain. If we support printing, list tested printer models (58 mm and 80 mm Bluetooth) explicitly.
- Offline claims (Rista "offline for days", Vyapar Table, BoltBills, RasaBill) are seller-asserted. I found no reviews confirming or refuting them, so offline is a table-stakes claim rather than a proven differentiator.

### Gaps
- Payment-settlement delays (T+1/T+2) and data loss did not appear in the snippets I could access, so I cannot confirm whether they are common complaints in these listings.
- Internet-outage complaints specific to cloud POS (Petpooja, Rista) did not surface. That may be a sampling limitation.
- Hardware lock-in and AMC complaints for sales-led POS vendors (Petpooja, Restroworks) were not visible in Play reviews. Such vendors' Play apps are companion apps, so those complaints probably live on Google Maps, Justdial or Reddit, which were not accessible.

### Inferences
- For a small-stall QR ordering product, the review evidence points to a minimal "must-not-fail" set:
  - Loud, reliable new-order alerts.
  - A one-tap "item out of stock" toggle that actually syncs to the customer menu (Rista's failure here was called out).
  - Today and yesterday sales on the home screen.
  - Payment-mode split (cash or UPI) in reports.
  - A UPI QR on the bill or the order screen.
  - Optional Bluetooth 58/80 mm KOT print with a published compatibility list.
- Trust features are a differentiator at our price point: a visible price with no setup fee, a trial end date shown in the app, cancel in-app, and a stated support SLA (for example, a WhatsApp reply within X hours). These are cheap to build and directly answer the most common 1★ themes across Petpooja, DotPe, Zomato, Swiggy, Vyapar and Zobaze.
- Avoid copying aggregator-style monetisation (ads wallets, auto-applied discounts, onboarding fees). Swiggy and Zomato reviews show these generate the angriest reviews.

### Gaps
- I could not quantify request frequency (for example, the share of reviews mentioning KOT) because full review exports could not be fetched. The ranking above is qualitative, based on how many apps and snapshots a theme appeared in.
- No Indian QR-ordering-specific app had enough reviews to extract diner-side or vendor-side feature requests about QR ordering itself (table QR, pay-at-counter versus prepaid, and so on).
